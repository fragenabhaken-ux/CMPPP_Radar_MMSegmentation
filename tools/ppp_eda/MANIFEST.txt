Complete PPP EDA implementation with RCS and spatial-domain evaluation

configs/ppp_eda/ppp_eda.py
  Full updated EDA config with ppp_eda_spatial.

tools/ppp_eda/ppp_eda_geometry.py
  Shared optional-domain configuration, image-to-BEV back-projection,
  Cartesian/polar conversion, masks, and automatic plot limits.

tools/ppp_eda/scan_ppp_eda.py
  Exports camera points, depth, velocity, RCS, Cartesian BEV coordinates,
  and polar coordinates.

tools/ppp_eda/render_ppp_examples.py
  Keeps camera overlays and adds Cartesian BEV and polar target plots.

tools/ppp_eda/plot_ppp_eda_summary.py
  Adds Cartesian and polar density summaries.

tools/ppp_eda/compare_ppp_eda_splits.py
  Adds train-validation Cartesian and polar density comparisons.

tools/ppp_eda/render_ppp_demo_sequence.py
  Adds Cartesian and polar outputs for the demo sequence.

tools/ppp_eda/render_ppp_demo_temporal_accumulation.py
  Adds Cartesian and polar temporal accumulation outputs.

tools/ppp_eda/select_ppp_examples.py
  Includes RCS frame statistics and high_rcs/low_rcs categories.

mmseg/datasets/transforms/sys100_ppp_transforms.py
  Four-channel camera target: occupancy, depth, velocity, RCS.

tests/test_ppp_eda_geometry.py
  Geometry and optional-limit tests.
